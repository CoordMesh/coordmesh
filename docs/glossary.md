# Glossary

Terms here are working language, not protocol definitions.

- **Coordination:** aligning actions, resources, capabilities, time, access, obligations, or decisions among participants.
- **Mechanism:** a general behavior or representation that applications may compose.
- **Institution:** a social or legal arrangement, such as a company or cooperative; not a foundational protocol concept.
- **REUSE:** an existing standard/project adequately addresses a capability and is used directly.
- **PROFILE:** an existing standard is used with documented constraints or additional shared semantics.
- **INVENT:** a new OpenCoord mechanism is justified by a documented unmet need.
- **UNKNOWN:** evidence is insufficient for classification.
- **CommonCell:** a possible future reference application for local voluntary coordination; not OpenCoord itself.
- **Three Neighbors:** a scenario for testing future specifications; not a complete requirements statement.
