# Vision

OpenCoord explores open, reusable infrastructure for decentralized coordination among people, organizations, software agents, and machines. Coordination spans needs, resources, capabilities, time, access, rights, responsibilities, agreements, decisions, and economic activity.

Many of these mechanisms are embedded in centralized applications or institution-specific systems. OpenCoord asks whether composable, interoperable mechanisms can let diverse applications coordinate while preserving their own institutional choices.

The aim is not a new state, government, company, cooperative, social network, or marketplace. A possible future application, CommonCell, may demonstrate a small voluntary local coordination unit; it is not the protocol.

OpenCoord should be useful in normal life. Decentralization may also support local continuity during partial disruption, island operation where possible, and reconnection and synchronization during recovery. Coexistence with existing legal, commercial, and public infrastructure is expected.

Long-term success means an independent builder can create an application the original contributors did not anticipate, using interoperable OpenCoord mechanisms. The project should progressively dogfood its own coordination while remaining able to continue without its founder.
