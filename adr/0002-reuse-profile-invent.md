# ADR 0002: REUSE / PROFILE / INVENT policy

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-10-07

## Context

OpenCoord should integrate existing technology where adequate and avoid novel protocol mechanisms without evidence of a gap.

## Proposal

Classify each capability as REUSE, PROFILE, INVENT, or UNKNOWN/MORE RESEARCH. An INVENT choice requires a documented gap and justification. Never invent cryptography.

## Consequences

Research evidence and limitations become explicit. Some capabilities may remain UNKNOWN until a scenario demonstrates need.
